# Idea gate (stub)

**When:** before committing time to build anything.

## Business questions
- Who exactly has this problem, and what does it cost them today (time, money, risk)?
- What do they use instead right now? Why would they switch?
- Would they pay, and roughly how much? What's the evidence (not opinion)?
- Why you — what unfair advantage do you have?

## Technical questions
- Is it technically possible with what exists? What's the single riskiest unknown?
- Which platform/API dependency could kill this (terms of service, rate limits, a vendor change)?

## Verdict
PASS / HOLD / KILL with evidence for each question. Stub: questions only — evidence rules come in a later version.
