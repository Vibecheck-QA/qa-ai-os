# Operate gate (stub)

**When:** continuously once customers rely on it.

## Business questions
- Are customers getting the outcome they paid for? How do you know without asking?
- What do support requests say the product gets wrong?

## Technical questions
- Does every silent failure have an alert, and does each alert work when the thing it reports on is broken?
- Are credentials, sessions and certificates monitored before they expire?
- Is there a health view a non-engineer can read?

## Verdict
PASS / HOLD / KILL with evidence for each question. Stub: questions only — evidence rules come in a later version.
