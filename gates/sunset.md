# Sunset / sale gate (stub)

**When:** before shutting down, selling, or handing over.

## Business questions
- Exit math: what is it worth, to whom, and why now?
- How are customers told, migrated, and refunded? What promises must be kept?

## Technical questions
- Data export for customers; deletion or transfer plan that meets your legal obligations.
- Handover runbook: every credential, service, domain, and scheduled job listed with an owner.
- Due-diligence pack: architecture, known debt, incident history, test and gate evidence.

## Verdict
PASS / HOLD / KILL with evidence for each question. Stub: questions only — evidence rules come in a later version.
